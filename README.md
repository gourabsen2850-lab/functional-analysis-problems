# Functional-Analysis-Problems and Notes
This repository contains a collection of problems, solution sketches,
and remarks in functional analysis.

The purpose of these notes is:
- self-study and consolidation of theory
- discussion of standard and non-standard problems
- exploration of ideas from textbooks and research papers

---

## Topics (planned)
- Normed and Banach spaces
- Hilbert spaces
- Bounded linear operators
- Weak convergence
- Hahn–Banach theorem
- Applications to PDEs

---

## Disclaimer

These notes are exploratory and written for learning and discussion.
Some arguments may be incomplete or incorrect.
Corrections and suggestions are welcome via Issues.
